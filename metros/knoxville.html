How it works

A building permit is a signed announcement: this homeowner is about to spend money on a project. PermitLocal turns that public filing into your lead list.

1. We pull the permits

Every day we collect the metro's newest building permits straight from official city and county data.

2. We match your work

Tell us your trade and the ZIP codes you cover. We tag every permit and keep only your matches — homeowner name, address, and job value included.

3. You get the digest

Fresh plan: one email at 6:30 AM, the same morning permits are filed. Delayed plan: a weekly email with leads 14 days after filing.
