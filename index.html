How it works

A building permit is a signed announcement: this homeowner is about to spend money on a project. PermitLocal turns that public filing into your lead list.

Step 1

We pull the permits

Every day we collect Fort Worth's newest building permits straight from the city's own data — the same morning they're filed.

Step 2

We match your work

Tell us your trade and the ZIP codes you cover. We tag every permit — fence, pool, landscaping, gutters, blinds — and keep only your matches.

Step 3

You get the digest

At 6:30 AM your matches land in one email: property address, homeowner name, type of work, and the job's value. Call first, win the job.
